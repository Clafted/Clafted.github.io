What is Computer Graphics?

Simply put, Computer Graphics is the field of Computer Science concerning the visualization of data. This data could represent numerous things, including geography, structures, molecules, sound (see the projects page), statistics, et cetera. Along with video-games and CGI, Computer Graphics is the foundation of simulation; CG powers data visualizers, CAD (Computer-Aided Design), driving and flight simulators, graphics editors (e.g. Blender), and many more.
